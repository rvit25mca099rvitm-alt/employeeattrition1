Why This Algorithm Is Used in This Project
1. High Sensitivity to Class Imbalance (Recall: {{ (res.recall * 100) | round(2) }}%)

Employee attrition is inherently imbalanced (~16% turnover rate). By using balanced class weights, the model prioritizes identifying true turnover risks, detecting over 70% of departing employees before they leave.

2. Direct SHAP Explainability & Transparency

Logistic Regression guarantees linear additivity in log-odds space, allowing SHAP LinearExplainer to calculate exact, game-theoretic individual feature contributions without approximation errors.

3. Ethical and Fair HR Governance

Unlike opaque complex models, Logistic Regression provides clear, explainable decision boundaries, ensuring HR decisions remain defensible, transparent, and fair.
